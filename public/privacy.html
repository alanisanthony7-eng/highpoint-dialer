Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026 · Version 2026-10-07

This policy explains what information the Highpoint desk (the "Service"), operated by Highpoint Financial LLC ("Highpoint," "we," "us"), collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers two kinds of information: information about agents who use the Service, and information about the leads and clients agents store in it.

1. Information we collect

About agents

  • Account details: name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), role, team and plan.
  • Billing details: subscription status and history. Card numbers are entered directly with our payment processor, Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Usage and device information: sign-in times, presence in the team lobby, settings, and basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser) used for security and troubleshooting.

About leads and clients (entered by agents)

  • Contact details, demographics such as age and date of birth, coverage interests, notes, quotes, appointments and pipeline stage.
  • Health and underwriting information an agent records, such as conditions, medications, height and weight.
  • Call and text records: numbers, times, outcomes, notes, and, where the agent turns them on, call recordings, voicemails, transcripts and summaries.

For lead and client information, the agent (and their agency) decides what is collected and why. We process it on their behalf to provide the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Service: sign-in, saving leads, placing calls and texts, quoting, scheduling and the Highpoint Bot.
  • To bill subscriptions and manage team access.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and back up data.
  • To improve features, using aggregated or de-identified information where possible.
  • To comply with law and respond to valid legal requests.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use lead or client information for our own marketing.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, under agreements that limit their use to providing that service: Cloudflare (hosting and database), Telnyx or other telephone carriers (calls, numbers and texts), Stripe (payments), and AI providers such as Anthropic (the Highpoint Bot and call summaries; text sent to the assistant is processed to generate a reply). Your team's admins can see team-level information such as agent names, roles, plans, billing status and last sign-in, but not other agents' private leads. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business.

4. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, private per-agent lead storage, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices. If we learn of a security incident affecting personal information, we'll notify affected users as required by law.

5. Retention

We keep account and lead information while the account is active. After an account ends, we keep data for a reasonable period so the agent or agency can export or transfer it, then delete it, unless we must keep it longer by law. Call recordings and transcripts follow the same rule unless deleted sooner by the agent.

6. Your choices and rights

Agents can view and update their account and lead information in the Service and can ask us to delete their account. Depending on where you live (for example under the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete personal information and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. Leads and clients whose information an agent stored should contact that agent or agency first; we'll help them respond. To make a request, email us at the address below. We'll verify your identity before acting on it.

7. Health information

Agents may record health details to screen insurance eligibility. The Service isn't a medical record system. Agents are responsible for collecting this information with the client's knowledge and for any obligations under insurance privacy laws that apply to them.

8. Children

The Service is for licensed professionals and isn't directed to children under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is material we'll ask agents to agree again in the Service.
